Bench Calc

Four calculators for the numbers that come up at a build bench. Change any value and the results update.

Size a stepper motor that drives a carriage on a lead screw, such as a T8 screw on a 3D printer axis or a small CNC.

How it is calculated 
Force      F = m × (a + g × μ)
Torque     T = F × lead / (2π × efficiency)
Required   T × safety factor
Step rate  RPM / 60 × steps × microsteps
Speed      RPM × lead / 60
Resolution lead / (steps × microsteps)

Work out the ratio, output speed and output torque of one or two gear stages. Leave stage 2 at 1 and 1 to use a single stage.

How it is calculated 
Stage ratio   driven teeth / driver teeth
Total ratio   stage 1 × stage 2
Output speed  input RPM / total ratio
Output torque input torque × total ratio × efficiency^meshes

Pick the series resistor for an LED. The suggested value is the next standard E24 resistor at or above the exact result, so the LED never runs over its target current.

How it is calculated 
R exact   (supply − forward voltage) / current
R used    next E24 value ≥ R exact
Current   (supply − forward voltage) / R used
Power     current² × R used

Find the output of a two-resistor divider, for example to scale a 12 V signal down for a 3.3 V microcontroller pin.

How it is calculated 
Output     Vin × R2 / (R1 + R2)
Current    Vin / (R1 + R2)
Power      Vin² / (R1 + R2)
Results are estimates for planning. Check motor datasheets and component ratings before you build.
